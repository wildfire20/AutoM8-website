---
name: School pricing model boundaries
description: Owner-approved distinctions between custom development, optional learner-band support and standard subscriptions.
---

Custom school systems use once-off development starting prices and optional flat learner-band support after launch. Standard per-learner subscriptions are a separate product: never add custom learner-band support to those tiers automatically. General website maintenance pricing is unrelated.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requires separate commercial models and transparent external provider costs. Harmony is technical proof, not evidence of any current price or its commercial agreement.

**How to apply:** Count actively enrolled learners only, excluding archived, former, withdrawn learners and unenrolled applicants. Absence or parent portal inactivity does not lower the count. Permit temporary growth of up to 5% within a term; review before the next term and move to the next band if still above the current band. Support covers reasonable existing-system care, not unlimited support or substantial new development. External services and new development are quoted separately.
