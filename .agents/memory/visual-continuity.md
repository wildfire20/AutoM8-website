---
name: Visual continuity
description: The visual and structural baseline future AutoM8 website changes should preserve.
---

Keep the recognisable production AutoM8 visual language—dark navy typography, restrained electric-blue emphasis, alternating light surfaces, branded buttons, subtle card depth and restrained motion—without restoring the former long-form page structure.

**Why:** The concise information architecture, progressive disclosure, Solutions and Insights architecture improved usability and SEO, but removing the earlier visual contrast made the site feel generic and unfinished.

**How to apply:** Treat the public production site as a visual-continuity reference. Keep the current shorter commercial journeys, portfolio filters, four-step process, pricing hierarchy, canonical pages and SEO infrastructure while refining visual presentation. Preserve professional visual details such as technology logos, icons and section artwork unless a requested change specifically requires replacing them.
