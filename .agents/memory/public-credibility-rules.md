---
name: Public credibility rules
description: Durable guardrails for claims, authorship, proof and positioning on the AutoM8 public website.
---

Keep AutoM8 positioned as a growing, founder-led South African software and automation company with direct communication, practical technology, flexible pricing and phased builds. Never invent a founder name, company scale, client, project status, statistic, testimonial, address, technology experience or performance result. Use organisational authorship until a verified person's name and role are supplied. Qualify ownership, licensing and handover as project-agreement terms rather than making universal promises.

**Why:** Trust and search authority must come from verifiable services, transparent pricing and real project evidence. Unsupported claims create legal, credibility and search-quality risk.

**How to apply:** Before adding company details, technology claims, case-study outcomes, schema or author profiles, verify them from owner-supplied information or existing project evidence. List unknown details as missing rather than filling them in.
