What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that a website or a service embedded in it may store in a browser. Similar technologies can remember settings or help measure use. Your browser can usually show, restrict or delete stored cookies through its settings.

What AutoM8 currently states

AutoM8 does not claim that this website uses advertising cookies or a marketing-tracking programme. The pages may use ordinary browser functionality, such as scripts for the mobile navigation or page animation, which is not the same as a claim that a tracking cookie is set. We have not invented a cookie inventory or retention period here.

How to control cookies

You can adjust cookie and site-data controls in your browser, delete existing site data, or use browser privacy tools. Blocking some functionality may affect how a website or an external service works. If a future AutoM8 feature needs non-essential cookies, we should explain that use and provide any consent or controls required by applicable law.
