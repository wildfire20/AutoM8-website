FAQ

Questions about school management systems.

How much does a school management system cost?

Pricing starts from R15 per learner per month for the base plan. The pro plan is R25 and the premium plan is R35 per learner per month. Final pricing is confirmed after discussing your school's specific requirements.

Does the system work for small schools?

Yes. The system is designed for schools of various sizes, including small private schools and tutoring centres. The base plan provides essential features at an accessible price point.

Can parents access the system?

Yes. The parent communication portal allows parents to view their child's attendance, grades and school announcements through a simple, secure interface.
