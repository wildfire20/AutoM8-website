Project overview

Several customer journeys, one platform.

AutoM8 developed a custom dynamic platform for Stats Companies that brings booking, rental and ordering functionality into one web-based system. The solution also includes customer account functionality and an administrative dashboard for managing platform activity.

Customer experience

Built around how customers interact.

The responsive interface helps customers move between services, products, bookings and account functions without relying on disconnected tools.

Online booking

Customers can access booking journeys from the same public platform used to explore the company’s services.

Rental workflows

Rental activity is handled as a dedicated workflow within the broader business platform.

Product ordering

Ordering functionality connects the public catalogue and customer actions to database-backed management.

Accounts & administration

Customer access with central management.

Customer accounts

  • Account authentication
  • Sign-in and customer access
  • Customer-facing booking and ordering journeys
  • Responsive desktop and mobile interface

Administrative dashboard

  • Central platform activity management
  • Booking oversight
  • Rental and ordering workflow management
  • Database-backed application data
Platform structure

A connected custom web application.

The project demonstrates how a public business website can also support transactional workflows, authenticated customer functions and internal management tools.
