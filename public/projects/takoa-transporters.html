Project overview

Transport information and enquiries in one clear customer journey.

AutoM8 developed a modern public website for TAKOA Transporters to give customers a clearer way to understand the company’s transport services, explore suitable vehicle categories and submit transport requests online. The platform supports TAKOA’s different service areas while giving customers clear routes into quote requests and direct WhatsApp communication.

Fleet showcase

Different journeys need different vehicles.

The fleet showcase helps customers understand the types of vehicles available for different group sizes and travel requirements before submitting an enquiry. Vehicle capacity and availability are confirmed by TAKOA.

Minibus Taxi

A category for smaller groups and flexible local journeys.

Midibus

A middle-ground category for organised groups travelling together.

Commuter Bus

Group passenger transport for journeys that need more room.

Luxury Coach

A coach category suited to planned group journeys and longer travel.

Quote & enquiry journey

From service discovery to a clear next step.

Structured requests collect the details TAKOA needs to review an enquiry. A direct WhatsApp route is also available throughout the website.

01

Discover

Customers explore TAKOA's services.

02

Choose

They identify the transport or travel service that fits their requirements.

03

Request

They submit their journey and contact details through the relevant enquiry process.

04

Respond

TAKOA receives the request and can continue the conversation, confirming availability directly.
