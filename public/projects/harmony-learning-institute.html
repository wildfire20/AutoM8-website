Project overview

One platform designed around the school.

Harmony Learning Institute is a private school with a fully custom digital platform designed specifically around the way the school operates. AutoM8 developed a connected system that brings together the school website, admissions, administration, parent communication, learner information, attendance, fees, documents, notifications and day-to-day school operations in one platform.
