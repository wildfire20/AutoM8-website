Parent portal features

What parents can access.

Attendance Records

Parents can view their child's daily attendance record and receive notifications for absences where configured.

Academic Progress

Grades, marks and progress reports accessible to parents at any time — not just at the end of the term.

School Announcements

Circulars, event notices and school news published by staff are visible to parents through the portal.

Report Cards

Term report cards generated from the system and made available to parents digitally — reducing printing and distribution.

Learner Profile

Basic learner information and contact details maintained in a centralised profile accessible to authorised staff and parents.

Secure Access

Parent accounts are created and managed by the school. Each parent can only view their own child's information.

The school-side system

The parent portal is part of a complete school management system.

The parent-facing portal is built on top of AutoM8's school management system, which handles the school-side administration — capturing grades, recording attendance, publishing announcements and generating report cards.

What the school captures in the administration system is what parents see in the portal. You only need to enter information once.

FAQ

Common questions about parent portals.

Does the parent portal work on a phone?

Yes. The parent portal is mobile-responsive and designed to work on any device — parents can check their child's information from a smartphone, tablet or computer.

Is the parent portal part of the school management system?

Yes. The parent portal is the parent-facing component of the AutoM8 school management system. The school administration side handles data capture, and the portal makes it accessible to parents.

Can the school control what parents see?

Yes. The school controls which information is published and visible to parents. Unpublished grades or announcements are not visible until the school makes them available.

How much does a parent portal cost?

The parent portal is included in the AutoM8 school management system, which is priced per learner per month starting from R15. See the full pricing page for details.
