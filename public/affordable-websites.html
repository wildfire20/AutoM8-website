The challenge

Without a website, your business is invisible online.

Most people search online before contacting a business. If your business has no website — or a website that doesn't look professional — you're losing potential clients to competitors who do.

You don't need to spend R50,000 on a website. A clean, professional, mobile-responsive website that loads quickly and includes a clear call to action is enough to make a strong first impression.

  • Get found on Google — a properly optimised website helps local customers find your business when they search.
  • Works on every device — mobile-responsive so customers can contact you from their phone.
  • WhatsApp integration — a prominent WhatsApp button makes it easy for visitors to contact you instantly.
  • Build credibility — a professional website shows clients you are a legitimate, established business.

FAQ

Common questions about business websites.

How much does a business website cost in South Africa?

AutoM8's website packages start from R1,500 for a one-page site. A small business multi-page website typically starts from R3,500. Final pricing depends on scope, content and functionality.

Is hosting included?

Hosting and domain registration are not included in the listed prices. We can recommend suitable, affordable hosting options and quote for these separately.

Do I need to provide my own content?

For the Starter package, the client provides final text and images. For other packages, we can assist with structuring content — but the factual information about your business must come from you.

How long does a website take to build?

Project timelines depend on scope and how quickly the client can provide content and feedback. Timelines are agreed as part of the proposal before work begins.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every AutoM8 website includes basic on-page SEO. For stronger search visibility, the Growth package includes Search Console and Analytics preparation. Ranking on Google also depends on the quality and consistency of your content over time.

Can I update the website myself?

This depends on the technology used for your site. We discuss this during the discovery call and can recommend solutions that make updates easy, or include a maintenance arrangement.
