Hosting and domains

What happens after development?

Development fees and provider costs are kept separate so you can see what you are paying for.

WordPress hosting

WordPress websites can normally use traditional WordPress or shared hosting, which is often billed monthly or annually depending on the provider.

Custom / dynamic hosting

Custom applications may require application or cloud hosting, databases, object storage or other production infrastructure.

Domains are normally owned and paid for by the client. Hosting-provider fees are separate from AutoM8 development fees unless specifically included. FAQ

Common questions.

Are the prices shown guaranteed?

No. All prices shown are starting prices. Final pricing depends on scope, content, integrations and functionality. A detailed quote is always provided before work begins.

How does payment work?

Projects are typically split into milestones — a deposit to begin, a payment at an agreed midpoint, and a final payment on delivery. Exact payment terms depend on the project.

Is WordPress worse than a custom website?

No. They serve different purposes. WordPress is often the better and more affordable solution for normal business websites. Custom development is recommended when a business requires specialised functionality or workflows.

Why is a custom website more expensive?

Custom websites often require functionality to be designed and programmed specifically for the business, including databases, user accounts, dashboards, integrations and business logic.

Can I move from WordPress to a custom system later?

Yes. A business can begin with a WordPress website and introduce custom systems later as its requirements grow.

Do I need monthly website fees?

No. Website development is normally a once-off fee. You may self-manage your website or select an optional AutoM8 Website Care plan.

Who pays for hosting and the domain?

Hosting, domains and other third-party provider costs are normally paid separately by the client. AutoM8 can assist with setup and management where required.

Can WordPress have an online shop?

Yes. AutoM8 can implement WooCommerce-based online stores where WordPress is suitable. Payment-gateway fees, premium plugins and other provider costs remain separate.

Can you add automation to a WordPress website?

Yes. Automation is an independent AutoM8 service and can often integrate with WordPress forms, CRM systems, email, WhatsApp and other supported tools.

Do I own the code?

Yes. You receive full ownership of all custom code — complete source code and documentation is yours once the project is paid in full.

How long does a project take?

Project timelines depend on scope, complexity and client feedback. Timelines are agreed as part of the proposal before development begins.

Can I pay in phases?

Yes. Larger projects can often be broken into phases, starting with the most critical features and expanding over time. This is discussed during the discovery conversation.
