FAQ

Common questions.

Are the prices shown guaranteed?

No. All prices shown are starting prices. Final pricing depends on scope, content, integrations and functionality. A detailed quote is always provided before work begins.

How does payment work?

Projects are typically split into milestones — a deposit to begin, a payment at an agreed midpoint, and a final payment on delivery. Exact payment terms depend on the project.

Is hosting and domain included?

Hosting, domain registration and ongoing maintenance are not included in the listed prices. These are discussed and quoted separately depending on your requirements.

Do I own the code?

Yes. You receive full ownership of all custom code — complete source code and documentation is yours once the project is paid in full.

How long does a project take?

Project timelines depend on scope, complexity and client feedback. Timelines are agreed as part of the proposal before development begins.

Can I pay in phases?

Yes. Larger projects can often be broken into phases, starting with the most critical features and expanding over time. This is discussed during the discovery conversation.
