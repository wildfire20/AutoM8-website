9. DEFINE WHAT THE LEARNER COUNT MEANS
==================================================

Clearly state:

The learner count is based on ACTIVELY ENROLLED learners.

Do NOT count:

- archived learners
- former learners
- withdrawn learners
- applicants who have not yet enrolled

A learner being absent or a parent not using the portal does not reduce the
monthly learner count.

10. ADD A 5% BAND GRACE ALLOWANCE
==================================================

Avoid an unreasonable jump when a school temporarily moves slightly above a
band.

Add this rule:

“A school may temporarily exceed its current learner band by up to 5% during
the school term without an immediate price increase.

Learner numbers are reviewed before the next school term. If enrolment remains
above the band at that review, the next pricing band will apply from the new
term.”

Example:

A school in the 1–150 band may temporarily grow to approximately 157 learners
during the term before pricing is reviewed.

Do not overcomplicate this explanation.
