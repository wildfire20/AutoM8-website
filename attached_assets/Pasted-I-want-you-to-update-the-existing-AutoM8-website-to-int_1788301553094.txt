14. FAQ SECTION
==================================================

Add FAQ items such as:

Q: Is WordPress worse than a custom website?
A:
No. They serve different purposes. WordPress is often the better and more affordable solution for normal business websites. Custom development is recommended when the business requires specialised functionality or workflows.

Q: Why is a custom website more expensive?
A:
Custom websites often require functionality to be designed and programmed specifically for the business, including databases, user accounts, dashboards, integrations and business logic.

Q: Can I move from WordPress to a custom system later?
A:
Yes. A business can begin with a WordPress website and introduce custom systems later as its requirements grow.

Q: Do I need monthly website fees?
A:
No. Website development is normally a once-off fee. Clients may self-manage their website or select an optional AutoM8 Website Care plan.

Q: Who pays for hosting and the domain?
A:
Hosting, domains and other third-party provider costs are normally paid separately by the client. AutoM8 can assist with setup and management where required.

Q: Can WordPress have an online shop?
A:
Yes. AutoM8 can implement WooCommerce-based online stores where WordPress is suitable.

Q: Can you add automation to a WordPress website?
A:
Yes. Automation is an independent AutoM8 service and can often integrate with WordPress forms, CRM systems, email, WhatsApp and other supported tools.
