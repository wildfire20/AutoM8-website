3. ADD FLAGSHIP FEATURE:
SMART FEE RECONCILIATION
==================================================

This should be one of the most prominent sections on the entire School Management Systems page.

Do NOT hide it inside a normal feature list.

Create a large section.

Suggested label:
FLAGSHIP WORKFLOW

TITLE:
Smart School-Fee Reconciliation

SUBTITLE:
Turn a monthly bank statement into organised learner payment records.

Explain the intended workflow clearly:

1. Each learner is assigned a unique payment reference.
2. Parents use that reference when making EFT school-fee payments.
3. An authorised school staff member downloads the school's bank statement.
4. The statement is uploaded into the AutoM8 school system.
5. The system analyses transaction references.
6. Recognised learner references are matched to the appropriate learner account.
7. The payment amount is recorded against that learner.
8. The system updates the learner's fee position.

The system should be able to represent outcomes such as:

- Paid in full
- Partially paid
- Outstanding
- Overpaid / credit balance
- Unmatched payment requiring staff review

IMPORTANT:
Do not claim that every bank transaction will always be matched automatically.

Use wording such as:

"Transactions containing recognised learner references can be matched automatically, while unclear or unmatched transactions can be flagged for staff review."
